## 🌸 私について · About me

I'm **Hanahira** — a **Super Junior Programmer :)** from the **HanahiraNetwork** corner of the internet.

I'm a bit of a language magpie: most of the web stack has passed through my editor at some point —
**Bun**, **Go**, **TypeScript**, Node, and plenty more besides. What stays constant is the shape of
what I build: **small, fast services that run on my own hardware**, wrapped in **Docker**, talking to **MySQL**.

Away from the web I keep two other hobbies alive — **AI image tooling** (ComfyUI / Civitai extensions)
and **C++ on microcontrollers**, because sometimes you want the thing you wrote to physically blink.

- 🌿 **Polyglot by habit** — I reach for whichever language fits the job, not whichever one I used last
- 🏠 **Self-hosted by default** — Linux, Docker Compose, `ufw` in front, my own metal underneath
- 🎨 **AI tooling on the side** — downloaders, browsers and quality-of-life extensions for ComfyUI
- 🔌 **Hardware for fun** — C++ sketches, sensors, and the occasional soldering burn
- 📫 **Say hi** — [hanahira.dev](https://hanahira.dev/) · [@HanahiraDev](https://x.com/HanahiraDev)
